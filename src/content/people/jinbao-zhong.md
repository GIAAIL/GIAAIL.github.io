Jinbao Zhong works on spatial cognition and human–computer interaction in extended reality. His dissertation develops the "Fourth Wall" as a theory of spatial thresholds in XR and spatial computing: how representations travel between physical, virtual and hybrid environments, and what changes in the way space is read when they do. He teaches computational design and data visualisation at the Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts and co-runs the design studio SENW.
