June-Hao Hou's background spans applied mathematics, visual communication, computer-aided design and digital media. He holds a Doctor of Design (2006) and a Master of Design Studies (1999) from the Harvard Graduate School of Design, and an MA from the Institute of Applied Arts, National Chiao Tung University (1996). He has taught at the Graduate Institute of Architecture since 2007, served as its Director from 2017 to 2023, and has led the Architectural Informatics Lab since 2021.

His teaching and research cover architectural information systems, design computation, digital fabrication, mathematics and design, interdisciplinary innovation design, human–computer interaction, information visualisation and information design. Earlier work at NCTU included creative direction for the Eco-City living lab and interactive installations and performances shown in Cologne (2008), Leipzig (2011) and Vienna (2013).
