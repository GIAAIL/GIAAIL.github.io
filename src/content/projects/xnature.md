years: 2022–2024
summary: "A two-year art-and-technology research project on the discrete and continuous relationships between people and nature: from their separation, to their fusion, to new imaginings of nature, ending in a multisensory immersive environment. Its four strands were kinetic sound installations drawn from complex-systems physics, soft robotics for converting between senses, thermal perception and comfort, and a pneumatic sensory-substitution landscape. Works include the lab's Animabotany, EMILS and Echoes of the Land; the collaborator was the Yasuaki Kakehi Lab, University of Tokyo."
summary_zh: "兩年期科技藝術研究計畫，探索人與自然之間的離散與連續關係：從人與自然的分野、融合，到新的自然想像，最終以多重感官的沉浸式共感環境呈現。四個方向為複雜系統物理的動態聲響裝置、感官轉換的軟性機器人、熱感知與舒適度，以及氣動的感官替代沉浸地景。作品包括研究室的《非碳基植物學》、《異生命的演繹》與《大地回音》；合作單位為東京大學筧康明研究室。"
