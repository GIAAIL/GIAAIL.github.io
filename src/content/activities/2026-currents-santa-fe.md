The lab's kinetic installation Animabotany was shown in the Taiwan programme of CURRENTS 2026, presented by Taiwan Academy Houston (Ministry of Culture, Taiwan) and Relay Santa Fe. The work takes the composition and growth logic of natural organisms into mechanical linkages and flexible light strips, whose layered motion reads as coral-like, predatory tentacles. Kinetic art, 180 × 180 × 160 cm; motor, plastics, metal, LED; 2024.
