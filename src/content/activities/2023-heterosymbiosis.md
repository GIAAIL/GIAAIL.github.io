Group exhibition of the XNature project and related art-and-technology work, curated by 許峻誠 and organised by the NYCU Arts Center and Institute of Applied Arts, with the Graduate Institute of Architecture and Tainan National University of the Arts as co-organisers. The lab was one of nine participating teams.
