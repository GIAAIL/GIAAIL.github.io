The institute's first joint faculty–student exhibition. Hou showed two works: 城市構竹 (bamboo construction from a 120-day option studio, with Sheng-Kai Tseng and 陳乃君) and *Transient Materialization* (with 鄭基立). As Director of the Institute he also moderated the design forum 不期而遇 on 7 March 2020.
