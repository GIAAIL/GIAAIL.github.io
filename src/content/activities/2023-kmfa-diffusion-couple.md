Exhibition of the NSTC art-and-technology research programme, organised by the National Science and Technology Council with the Kaohsiung Museum of Fine Arts and co-organised by National Yunlin University of Science and Technology; nine funded projects took part. The XNature project, led by June-Hao Hou, showed the lab's Animabotany together with EMILS from the same project.
