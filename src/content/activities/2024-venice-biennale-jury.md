Hou sat on the seven-member jury that selected the proposal for the Taiwan Pavilion at the 2025 Venice Architecture Biennale, run by the National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts. The jury chose *Non-Belief* ([無]－信仰) from the Department of Architecture, National Cheng Kung University.
