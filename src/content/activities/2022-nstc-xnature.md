Two-year art-and-technology research project on discrete and continuous relationships between people and nature: from their separation, to their fusion, to new imaginings of nature, ending in a multisensory immersive environment. Its four strands were kinetic sound installations drawn from complex-systems physics, soft robotics for converting between senses, thermal perception and comfort, and a pneumatic sensory-substitution landscape. Works include the lab's Animabotany, EMILS and Echoes of the Land; the collaborator was the Yasuaki Kakehi Lab, University of Tokyo.
