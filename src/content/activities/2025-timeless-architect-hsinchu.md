Centennial exhibition for the 1925 Hsinchu City Hall building (新竹市役所), now the Hsinchu Art Museum. Historical photographs, building components, policy documents and contemporary artworks, including Chen-An Lee's *The Unfinished Column*, trace the building's change from administrative office to cultural landmark. Organised by the Cultural Affairs Bureau, Hsinchu City. In 2026 the Hsinchu City Government reported that the exhibition received an iF Design Award.
