The 31st International Conference of the Association for Computer-Aided Architectural Design Research in Asia, hosted by the NYCU Graduate Institute of Architecture, with workshops at NYCU, Feng Chia University and National Yunlin University of Science and Technology from 26 to 28 April. Keynotes by Jeffrey Huang, Yu-Tung Liu, Ying-Chao Kuo and Andrew Witt. It was the second CAADRIA held on this campus, after NCTU hosted CAADRIA '97.
